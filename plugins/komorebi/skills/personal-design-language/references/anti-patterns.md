# Usually avoid

These are warning signals, not absolute bans. Use one only when it solves a specific product, hierarchy, usability, narrative, or brand problem; state that reason in the plan or review and preserve the project’s established identity.

- Card-everywhere layouts, nested cards, and arbitrary surface layering.
- Pill-shaped controls everywhere, including pill tabs and filters.
- Generic purple-blue gradients, glassmorphism, glow, or oversized decorative shadow.
- Icon-in-a-colored-square treatment without a scanning or semantic benefit.
- Huge hero text without a specific message, fake metrics, meaningless badges, or fabricated social proof.
- Decorative blobs, emoji as interface decoration, and repetitive three-column feature grids.
- Excess whitespace with no narrative rhythm, or separators used where hierarchy and spacing would suffice.
- Repeated motion, gradients, textures, or visual effects that make a screen look generated rather than designed.
